# ERD of Military Management System

The Military Management System (MMS) focuses on managing military personnel, branches, units, ranks, missions, weapons, awards, and service records. 

-- Primary Entities:
- Soldier
- Branch
- Unit
- Rank
- Weapon
- Mission
- Award
- Service Record

-- Associative Entities (to resolve many-to-many relationships):
- Soldier_Weapon
- Soldier_Mission
- Soldier_Award
